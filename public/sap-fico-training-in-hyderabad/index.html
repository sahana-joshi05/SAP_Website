What is SAP FICO, and why is it in demand?

It stands for Financial Accounting (FI) and Controlling (CO), the finance modules of SAP ERP and SAP S/4HANA used across organisations for external reporting and internal cost control.

What is the difference between FI and CO?

FI is for external stakeholders such as auditors, tax authorities and investors, driven by legal requirements. CO is for internal management and tracks costs and segment profitability.

What is a chart of accounts and what types are there?

It is the list of G/L accounts used by one or more company codes: operating (daily postings), group (consolidation) and country-specific (local statutory requirements).

What is a field status group?

Assigned in the G/L master record, it controls which fields are required, optional, hidden or display-only during posting.

What is a reconciliation account?

A G/L account automatically updated when posting to a sub-ledger (vendor, customer or asset). Direct posting is blocked so sub-ledgers and the G/L remain synchronised.
