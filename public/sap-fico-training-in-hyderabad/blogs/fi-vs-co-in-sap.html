FI vs CO in SAP: Key Differences and How They Work Together

One of the first interview and classroom questions in SAP finance is: what separates FI from CO, and why are they almost always taught together as SAP FICO?

1. Financial Accounting (FI): External Reporting

FI is driven by statutory, legal and tax compliance. Its audience includes external auditors, tax authorities, regulators, banks and shareholders. Core sub-modules include General Ledger (FI-GL), Accounts Payable (FI-AP), Accounts Receivable (FI-AR), Asset Accounting (FI-AA) and Bank Accounting.

2. Controlling (CO): Internal Cost Management

CO is designed for internal management teams. It answers operational questions such as which department spent over budget, what an internal project costs, and how profitable specific products or regions are. Key components include Cost Elements, Cost Centers, Internal Orders, Profit Centers, Allocations, Product Costing and CO-PA.

3. How FI and CO Connect in Real Time

Whenever an expense or revenue is posted to a G/L account linked to a cost element in FI, SAP automatically passes the cost data to a CO cost object such as a cost center or internal order. In SAP S/4HANA, both FI and CO store line items together in the Universal Journal (ACDOCA), reducing manual reconciliation effort.
