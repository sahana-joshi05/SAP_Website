SAP FICO Interview Preparation Guide

Interviewers rarely test memorised definitions alone—they check whether you understand how enterprise configuration controls daily financial postings.

5 Core Concepts Every Candidate Should Explain Clearly

  1. FI vs CO: External statutory reporting versus internal cost and profitability tracking.
  2. Chart of Accounts Types: Operating (daily postings), Group (consolidation) and Country-specific (local legal reporting).
  3. Field Status Group: Assigned in the G/L master record to control required, optional, hidden or display-only fields during posting.
  4. Reconciliation Account: Automatically updated G/L account linked to vendor, customer or asset sub-ledgers that blocks direct manual postings.
  5. Integration Flows: How MM procurement (goods receipt and invoice verification) and SD billing trigger automatic FI postings.
