SAP FICO Syllabus Overview: What Each of the 12 Modules Covers

A well-structured SAP FICO syllabus connects foundational navigation with sub-ledger automation, controlling, logistics integration and reporting. Here is how the 12 modules build on one another.

Stage 1: Foundations & General Ledger (Modules 1–3)

You start with SAP landscape basics, SAP GUI and Fiori navigation, and then configure Company, Company Code, Controlling Area, Chart of Accounts, Fiscal Year/Posting Period variants and Field Status Groups before working through FI-GL postings, foreign currency valuation, document splitting and the Universal Journal.

Stage 2: Sub-Ledgers, Banking & Taxes (Modules 4–8)

Next, you configure vendor and customer cycles in FI-AP and FI-AR (including APP, dunning and TDS), manage asset classes and depreciation in FI-AA, set up house banks and reconciliation in Bank Accounting, and configure GST/tax codes under Indirect Taxes.

Stage 3: Controlling, Integration & Readiness (Modules 9–12)

Finally, you configure CO cost centers, internal orders and profit centers, map MM-FI, SD-FI and PP-CO automatic account determination, build Fiori and drill-down financial statements, and complete end-to-end case study and interview preparation.
