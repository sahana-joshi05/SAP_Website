What Does an SAP FICO Consultant Do?

Unlike an accountant who primarily posts daily entries, an SAP FICO consultant designs, configures and supports the SAP system so finance teams can operate accurately and reliably.

Core Responsibilities

  • Requirement Gathering & Mapping: Discussing chart of accounts, tax rules, payment cycles and reporting needs with client finance teams.
  • System Configuration: Setting up company codes, posting period variants, field status groups, Automatic Payment Program (APP), dunning, asset classes, tax codes and controlling structures.
  • Cross-Module Coordination: Working with MM, SD and PP teams on automatic account determination so procurement, billing and production postings hit the right G/L accounts.
  • Testing & Month-End Support: Running integration tests, resolving daily support tickets, and supporting smooth month-end and year-end closing activities.

Entry Pathways

Freshers and finance executives often start as junior analysts, SAP end users or application support associates before progressing into full configuration and implementation responsibilities.
